1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the MediaKit app, website, and support service handle information. References to “we,” “us,” and “our” mean the developer providing MediaKit. The developer’s legal identity and location will be added before this policy takes effect.

MediaKit provides tools for compressing and converting media, stitching images, editing metadata, and protecting supported files and text. You do not need to create a MediaKit account.

2. Media and information on your device

Your media is processed on your device. The app does not upload your photos, videos, audio, PDF files, text, or encryption passwords to a MediaKit processing server.

  • Files you choose: the app reads selected media and creates working copies, previews, and output files to carry out your requested task.
  • Media metadata: depending on the tool, this may include file names, dimensions, formats, dates, author information, and location data already embedded in your media. Reading embedded location data does not mean that the app tracks your live location.
  • Preferences and recovery: the app stores settings such as your language, recently selected formats, and conversion settings locally. It can retain conversion queues, source files, and results so a session can be restored.
  • Text and passwords: password tools use the text, media, and password you provide to perform the operation locally. We do not operate a password recovery service.

The app does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Permissions, copying, and sharing

Photo library permission lets you select media and save supported results. You can limit or revoke this permission through your device’s Settings. Tools that support file import access the files you choose through the system file picker.

If an original is stored in iCloud or another file provider, the operating system or that provider may download it before processing. This network activity is separate from MediaKit’s local processing.

Copying places the selected result on the system clipboard. Sharing or exporting sends a copy to the destination you choose, such as Photos, Files, another app, or a cloud folder. That destination’s settings and policies apply to its copy. We cannot remove copies from other apps or recipients for you.

4. Storage and deletion

Different kinds of local data have different lifetimes:

  • Ordinary working files: at app startup, MediaKit attempts to remove ordinary temporary files last modified more than seven days earlier. Cleanup depends on the app running and the operation succeeding; this is not a guaranteed deletion deadline.
  • Conversion recovery: files referenced by a saved conversion session can remain longer so you can resume work. Recovery storage is marked to be excluded from device backups. Removing a job updates the saved session; unused recovery files are eligible for cleanup on a subsequent app launch.
  • Password-protection working files: separate temporary files are released when no longer needed by the operation, preview, or export flow. The app also attempts to clean up leftovers from earlier runs when it starts again.
  • Preferences: local settings remain until changed or the app’s data is removed.

Deleting the app through iOS removes its local app data; offloading the app may retain documents and data. Exported files, Photos items, cloud copies, backups, and email messages are separate and must be managed in their respective apps or services. Do not rely on MediaKit to securely erase every copy of a file.

5. Apple services and purchases

If purchases are available, Apple processes payment through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit transaction and subscription-status information to check access, restore purchases, and respond to expiry or revocation. We do not receive your full payment-card details through the app.

Apple’s handling of App Store, iCloud, and device-service information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy . Purchase availability and terms are shown before you confirm an available offer.

6. Website hosting and cookies

This website uses static HTML and CSS. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, embedded third-party media, or browser storage for visitor profiling.

When this website is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This hosting activity is separate from media processing in the app. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

Following an external link takes you to another provider’s service, where its own privacy practices apply.

7. Support emails and diagnostics

If you email support@mediaprocess.com , we receive your email address, the message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request, investigate the issue, and maintain relevant support records.

Please send only the details needed to explain the problem. Do not send encryption passwords, payment-card information, or private source media. If a screenshot or diagnostic excerpt is useful, remove unrelated personal information first.

The app can write local processing-error diagnostics to device logs. It does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting upload service. Device-level diagnostic sharing is controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Support correspondence is handled through email services and kept only as needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-ups, and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. You can contact us to request deletion of information we hold, subject to those requirements.

8. Service providers and disclosures

Apple, GitHub, and email service providers handle information needed to deliver their respective services. Information you share with a third-party app or cloud destination is also handled by that destination. These providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

We limit access to support information to what is needed to handle requests and operate the service. We may disclose information we hold if legally required or as necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims. This does not give us access to media that remains only on your device.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage photo access in device Settings, choose what to import or share, change local preferences, and remove exported files in their destination apps.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a relevant data-protection authority.

Send requests to support@mediaprocess.com . We may ask for proportionate information to verify that a request concerns your data. We cannot retrieve or delete files stored only on your device, or change records held independently by Apple or GitHub. We will explain any applicable limitations when responding.

10. Children and families

MediaKit is intended for users aged 4 and older . Children under 4 should not use the service. An age recommendation does not replace the permission or supervision required by local law.

A parent or legal guardian should supervise a minor’s use, including file sharing, support messages, and purchases, and provide any consent required by applicable law. We do not knowingly seek personal information directly from children where required parental authorization has not been obtained.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information without the required authorization, contact support@mediaprocess.com so we can review and address the situation, including deleting information where appropriate.

11. Security and protection limits

Local processing reduces the need to transfer your media, but no device or method of storage is completely secure. Protect your device, keep backups you control, and share files carefully.

Text password encryption and audio/video password protection are distinct from image scrambling and Emoji fun encoding. Image scrambling is not equivalent to authenticated file encryption, and Emoji fun encoding offers no confidentiality. We cannot recover forgotten encryption passwords or control copies you share elsewhere.

12. Updates and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and show the revised date. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent for a material change, we will provide it before applying that change. This draft will receive an effective date before public launch.

For privacy questions or requests, email support@mediaprocess.com . For practical help with the app, visit Support .
