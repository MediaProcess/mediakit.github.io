01 / THE BASICS

Getting started

Which devices are supported?

MediaKit is being prepared for iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Check the App Store listing for final device compatibility when it becomes available.

Do I need to create an account?

No. MediaKit does not require a MediaKit account to use its media tools. Any App Store purchase or restore uses your Apple Account.

Which languages does the app support?

The app includes English, Simplified Chinese, French, Russian, Japanese, Spanish, and German. Choose a language in the app’s Settings, or follow your device language. This website is currently available in English.

Where can I download MediaKit?

MediaKit is coming soon to the App Store. The download link will appear on our homepage when it is available.

02 / YOUR MEDIA

Working with files

Which file formats can I convert?

MediaKit includes image, video, and audio conversion tools. Common output formats include JPEG, PNG, MP4, MOV, MP3, and WAV. Dedicated tools handle GIFs, Live Photos, and PDF pages exported as images.

Available options depend on the selected tool and source file. A file extension alone does not guarantee compatibility: the media codec and file contents also matter. Check the format options shown in the app.

Why is my exported file larger than the original?

File size depends on dimensions, quality, format, and the source media. An already compressed file may not get smaller, and a lossless output can be larger.

Try a lower quality setting or smaller dimensions where available, then compare the result before saving. Keep your original until you are happy with the output.

Why can’t I see all my photos?

Your photo permission may allow access only to selected items. Update the selection or photo access for MediaKit in your iPhone’s Settings. Photos stored only in iCloud may also need to download before they can be processed.

Where do my exported files go?

The destination depends on the action you choose. Save to Photos adds supported media to your photo library. Save to Files lets you choose a folder. Sharing passes the output to the app or destination you select.

Wait for the save or export confirmation before leaving the flow.

What can I try if processing fails?

Check available device storage and make sure the original file can be opened. If it is stored in iCloud, let it finish downloading. Retry with a supported format or a smaller file to help identify the cause.

If the issue continues, note the tool, file type, and error message, then contact support .

03 / PEACE OF MIND

Privacy & protection

Does MediaKit upload my media for processing?

Media processing runs on your device; MediaKit does not use a cloud processing service for these tools.

This does not mean every action is offline. Fetching an iCloud original, making or restoring a purchase, and sharing to another service may use the network. Files you export or share are handled by the destination you choose.

Why does MediaKit need photo library access?

Photo access lets you select media from your library and save supported results back to Photos. You control that permission in your iPhone’s Settings. You can also use file import where a tool supports it.

Are all the protection tools the same?

No. Text password encryption and audio/video password protection use authenticated encryption. Protected audio and video are exported as password-protected files that need to be decrypted before normal playback.

Image scrambling changes how an image looks and is not equivalent to those encryption tools. Emoji fun encoding is a playful way to represent text and provides no confidentiality. Choose the tool that matches your needs.

Can support recover a forgotten encryption password?

There is no support recovery mechanism for text or media files protected with password encryption. Keep your password somewhere safe and retain any original you may need. Never send your password to support.

04 / PURCHASE HELP

Purchases

How do I restore a purchase?

If you have purchased an available App Store plan, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Open the app’s subscription screen from Settings and choose Restore. Restoring a purchase needs an internet connection.

MediaKit is currently preparing for release. Available plans and their details will be shown in the app at launch.

Where can I see pricing and subscription details?

When MediaKit launches, check the app’s purchase screen for available plans, local prices, billing periods, and any eligible offers before confirming a purchase.
